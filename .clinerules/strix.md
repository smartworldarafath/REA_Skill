# Strix AI Penetration Testing (Cline Rule)

Skill: `strix` — `skills/strix/SKILL.md` / `.agents/skills/strix/SKILL.md`

Autonomous AI penetration testing agent (usestrix/strix) for owner-authorized vulnerability discovery and remediation.

- Authorized targets only: systems you own or have explicit, written permission to test, within agreed scope.
- No third-party targets; unauthorized testing is illegal in most jurisdictions.
- Combine Strix scan results with REA skills (`website-security-audit`, `rea-binary-disassembly`, `rea-full-code-recovery`, etc.) to analyze and fix findings.
- Strix is "as is"; user bears responsibility for legal compliance.
